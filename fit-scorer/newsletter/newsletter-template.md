# newsletter-template.md

Copy to `newsletter/YYYY-MM-<client-name>.md`. Drafts land here only after
human review; nothing is ever sent from this repo. Format and cadence are
Grant's call (spec §17.1) — this template is a placeholder shape until he
confirms.

A newsletter with no threshold-clearing products still ships if it carries
relevant news; it just carries no recommendation that cycle. Recommendations
appear only when something clears the bar — scarcity keeps them meaningful.

```markdown
# <Client> — <Month YYYY>

Status: draft — pending human review | reviewed by <name> YYYY-MM-DD

## News and context for your stack
<!-- Relevant news for the client's stack (newsjack signal feed adds value
here). Ownership of drafting this section is an open decision, spec §17.1. -->

## Recommendation (only if a run cleared threshold + review)
<!-- Use the client-facing recommendation block from scorecard-template.md:
what it is · what it solves for them specifically · why GPC recommends it ·
what it costs (flagged if estimated). Partner disclosure when applicable +
standing disclaimer, both non-omittable. -->

## On our watchlist
<!-- Optional: named items being tracked, with what-would-change triggers. -->
```
